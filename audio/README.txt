LIVE DIALOGUE AUDIO
Record one short clip of the demo conversation and save it here as:
    live-dialogue.mp3   (about 6-8 seconds, MP3)

Suggested script (three voices, natural pace, ~1.6s between lines):
  Hub:      "Margaret, I noticed a fall. Are you okay?"
  Margaret: "I slipped by the couch and I can't get up."
  Family:   "I can hear you, Mom - stay still, help is coming."

Once this file is in place, a "Sound on" control appears on the demo
automatically and the audio plays when someone presses Play.
