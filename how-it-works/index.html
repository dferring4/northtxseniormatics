How SeniorMatics works.

Three quiet steps stand between a fall and help that already understands the situation — with no cameras and nothing to wear.

Radar notices a fall

Medical-grade radar continuously senses movement and position throughout the room. It sees a fall the moment it happens — without a single camera, and without any pendant or button your loved one has to wear or remember.

A conversation opens

Instead of firing a silent alarm, the hub automatically opens a live, two-way voice connection. Someone can speak with your loved one right away — to reassure them and understand exactly what happened.

Help arrives with context

Because the situation is understood in real time, the right help is sent with the full story — not just a location and a guess. Everyone acts faster and calmer.

What makes SeniorMatics different

Most alerts just beep. Ours starts a conversation.

When a fall is detected, a silent alarm only tells you that something happened. SeniorMatics opens a live, two-way voice connection automatically — so you hear what’s wrong, reassure your loved one, and send help with the full story.

Quietly dependable

Built to keep watch, even when things go wrong.

  • No cameras, ever. Radar senses movement and position without capturing a single image, so privacy and dignity stay intact.
  • Nothing to wear. No pendants or wristbands to charge, forget, or resist.
  • Works through outages. Local intelligence and battery backup keep the system vigilant during temporary internet or power interruptions.
  • Fewer false alarms. The AI qualifies what actually happened before anyone is alerted, reducing alarm fatigue.
