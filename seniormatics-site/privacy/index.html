Privacy policy.

How North TX SeniorMatics handles the information you share with us.

This is a starting template. Please have it reviewed to make sure it reflects your actual practices and any legal requirements before launch.

Information we collect

When you contact us or request a consultation, we collect the details you provide — such as your name, phone number, email address, city, and any message you send. We may also collect basic, non-identifying analytics about how visitors use this website.

How we use your information

We use your information solely to respond to your inquiry, provide the services you ask about, and improve our website. We do not sell your personal information.

Sharing

We may share information with service providers who help us operate (for example, the platform that delivers our contact form) and with our technology partner, Grand-App AI, as needed to provide SeniorMatics services. We do not share your information for unrelated marketing.

Analytics

This site may use analytics tools to understand traffic and improve the experience. These tools may set cookies in your browser.
