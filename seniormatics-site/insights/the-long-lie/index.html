The “Long Lie”: Why a Fall Is More Than Just an Injury

Safety Insights · February 18, 2026

When we picture a fall, we picture the impact. But for older adults living alone, the most dangerous part often isn’t the fall itself — it’s the time spent on the floor afterward, unable to get up or call for help. Clinicians call it the “long lie.”

Why time on the floor matters

The longer someone remains down, the greater the risk of serious complications — on top of the fear and helplessness of waiting alone. A relatively minor fall can become a genuine medical emergency simply because no one knew in time.

Closing the gap

This is where a system that responds instantly makes the real difference. SeniorMatics radar detects a fall the moment it happens, and TalkAssist opens a live two-way conversation right away — so your loved one hears a calm voice and help is on its way in seconds, not hours.

A fall may be unavoidable. A long lie doesn’t have to be.
