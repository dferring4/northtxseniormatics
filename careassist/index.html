How CareAssist protects

Smart sensing, no cameras.

  • Radar technology. Sophisticated sensors monitor for falls around the clock — no cameras, no wearables.
  • Fall qualification. After a fall is detected, the AI-powered hub engages the resident to qualify the event instantly.
  • Intelligence engine. Data feeds directly into the SeniorMatics AI to reduce alarm fatigue and false alerts.
