What is SeniorMatics?

SeniorMatics helps seniors live safely and confidently while giving families peace of mind. The easy-to-use technology provides practical support and resources designed to help older adults maintain independence and stay connected.

Agents use SeniorMatics to create meaningful conversations about protection, retirement, and quality of life. Families appreciate having a trusted advisor who offers solutions beyond traditional insurance products.

Partner FAQ

Questions from agents & brokers.

Who is the SeniorMatics partner program for?

Licensed insurance agents and brokers — especially those serving seniors and their families across North Texas — who want to offer clients more than a traditional policy.

What is SeniorMatics?

Privacy-first senior safety technology: radar-based fall detection and conversational hubs that help older adults age in place safely, with no cameras or wearables. It gives you a meaningful reason to talk with clients about protection, retirement, and quality of life.

How do I become a partner?

It’s simple: schedule a quick onboarding call, learn the product in minutes, receive your marketing materials and support, and start sharing SeniorMatics with clients.

What support do partners receive?

Onboarding, marketing materials, and ongoing support from our local, Flower Mound-based team.

Do I need experience with senior-care technology?

No. SeniorMatics is easy to learn and explain, and we provide everything you need to introduce it to clients with confidence.

What areas does North TX SeniorMatics serve?

The Dallas–Fort Worth metroplex and surrounding North Texas communities.
